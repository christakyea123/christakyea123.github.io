Roll Surge

Roll. Grow. Smash!

Steer your ball down a bright, twisting track, swallow orbs to grow bigger and heavier, and plough straight through number walls. Dodge saws and spinners, choose the smartest gate, chain combos into Fever mode and finish every level with a coin-multiplying bonus run.

Privacy Policy Contact Developer

How to Play

  • Drag left / right: steer the ball.
  • Eat orbs: every orb makes your ball bigger and heavier.
  • Smash walls: blocks with numbers lower than your size break apart; bigger ones shrink you.
  • Pick gates: choose the gate that grows you the most.
  • Fever: chain orbs and perfect smashes to fill the Fever bar, then smash everything for free.

Features

  • Endless levels with saws, spinners, gates and walls that get harder as you go.
  • Combos and Fever mode with praise, near-miss bonuses and 3-star level ratings.
  • Bonus finish: roll as far as you can to multiply your coins up to x10.
  • 12 ball skins to unlock, from Bubblegum to Royal Gold.
  • Daily gift: a growing reward for every day you play.
  • Plays offline: all gameplay and progress work without an internet connection.
