The short version

Traffic Escape Puzzle is a single-player offline puzzle game. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of its own. Your game progress never leaves your phone.

The game uses two Google services that need an internet connection:

  • Google AdMob, which shows ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK collects data as described below.
  • Google Play Billing, which handles the optional one-time Traffic Escape Pro purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your payment details.

If you never connect to the internet, the game still works in full — you simply see no ads, and Pro cannot be bought or restored until you are back online.

What we collect

Nothing. We operate no servers, no accounts and no analytics. We receive no personal information about you, and there is no mechanism in the app by which we could.

What the app stores on your device

The game keeps a small database in its private storage on your phone. It stays on the device, is not backed up to us, and is not readable by other apps. It contains only:

  • Your coin balance, lives, and the level you have reached
  • Per-level progress: whether a level is complete, its star rating, your best move count, attempts and hints used
  • Achievement progress and unlock times
  • Daily-reward streak and the date of your last claim
  • Lifetime play statistics (levels completed, total moves, coins earned, time played)
  • Your sound, music and vibration preferences, and the board theme you picked
  • Whether you own Traffic Escape Pro

None of this identifies you. It contains no name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, files, or precise location, because the app never asks for any of them.

You can erase all of it at any time from Settings → Reset Progress, or by uninstalling the app. Resetting progress keeps your Pro purchase; it is tied to your Google account and can always be restored.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and is paid for by ads. Owners of Traffic Escape Pro see no banner or interstitial ads at all. We use Google AdMob to serve banner, interstitial and rewarded ads. Rewarded ads are always optional — you choose to watch one in exchange for coins, a hint, or a life, and nothing in the game requires you to watch an ad to progress.

To serve and measure ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID)
  • Device information such as model, operating system version, language and screen size
  • IP address, from which an approximate (city-level) location can be derived
  • Ad interaction data — which ads were shown, viewed, or clicked

This processing is carried out by Google as an independent party, under its own policies, not by us. We never see this data in a form that identifies you; we see only aggregate earnings reports.

Your choices about ads

  • Consent: where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland) the game shows Google's consent message before any ad is requested. You can change your answer at any time from Settings → Privacy settings in the game.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. There you can reset the identifier or delete it entirely, which stops it being used to personalise ads.
  • Opt out of personalised ads: the same Android settings screen. You will still see ads, but they will not be personalised.
  • Play offline: with no network connection the ad SDK cannot load anything, and the game remains fully playable.

In-app purchase (Google Play Billing)

Traffic Escape Pro is an optional, one-time purchase (not a subscription). It removes banner and interstitial ads and unlocks unlimited lives and hints, Auto-Solve, double coins and extra board themes. Nothing in the game requires it.

The purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing, under Google's terms and privacy policy. We never receive your name, email address, card details or billing address. Google Play tells the app only that the Pro product was bought, along with a purchase confirmation, so the app can unlock Pro and store that fact on your device. We do not send that confirmation to any server of ours — we have none.

You can restore Pro on a new phone or after reinstalling from Settings → Restore purchases (on Android this also happens automatically when the game starts). Refunds and purchase history are handled by Google Play — see Google Play refunds and the Google Play Terms of Service.

Children

Traffic Escape Puzzle is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, it is not part of Google Play's Designed for Families programme, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

The app is suitable for a general audience of 13 and over: there is no violent, sexual, or otherwise mature content in the game itself. The age floor exists because the game shows advertising, and the advertising identifier used to serve those ads is not appropriate for children under 13.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has used this app, the game stores nothing we can see and nothing that identifies anyone — you can erase everything on the device through Settings → Reset Progress or by uninstalling. You can also reset or delete the device advertising identifier from Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. If you have any concern, contact us at christakyea123@gmail.com and we will act on it.

Data retention and deletion

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to retain or delete on our side.

Data stored on your device is under your control:

  • Settings → Reset Progress erases all game progress, coins and achievements immediately.
  • Uninstalling the app removes the database entirely.

For data held by Google in connection with advertising or purchases, use the Android advertising settings described above, or see Google's privacy policy for how to exercise your rights with them directly.

Security

Game data is stored in the app's private storage area, which Android isolates from other applications. Since no personal data is transmitted by the app, there is no transmission for us to secure. Ad and purchase traffic goes directly to Google over encrypted connections.

International transfers

We transfer nothing. Google may process advertising and purchase data on servers outside your country; see Google's privacy policy for details of its safeguards.
