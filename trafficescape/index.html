Traffic Escape Puzzle

Slide. Think. Escape!

The parking lot is jammed and the red car needs out. Slide the other cars along their lanes, open a path and drive it through the exit. Easy to learn, wonderfully hard to master, and playable anywhere — no internet needed.

Get it on Google Play Privacy Policy Contact Developer

How to Play

  • Drag a car along its lane. Cars only move forwards and backwards.
  • Clear the path between the red car and the green exit.
  • Escape in as few moves as you can to earn all three stars.
  • Stuck? Undo is free, and a hint lights up the next best move.

Features

  • 500 levels from Beginner to Expert, every one checked to be solvable.
  • Smart hints and free undo so a wrong move never costs you a star.
  • Daily rewards, achievements and stats to keep you coming back.
  • Plays offline: all gameplay and progress stay on your phone.
