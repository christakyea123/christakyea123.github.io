Privacy Policy

Mind Trap: The Impossible Puzzle · Last updated: 28 November 2026

This policy explains what data the Mind Trap mobile game ("Mind Trap", "the game", "we") handles and why. In short: your game data stays on your device. Mind Trap has no user accounts and no servers of its own, and we do not receive any information about you. The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. It also offers one optional, one-time in-app purchase, Mind Trap Pro, which is handled entirely by Google Play. Ads and the Pro purchase are the only parts of the game that use the internet.

1. Your game data

  • Your progress, levels, stars, rank, coins, times, achievements, Daily Challenge results and streaks, daily login rewards, Brain Blitz scores, chosen theme and settings are stored only on your device, in a local database inside the game's private storage.
  • Mind Trap adapts its puzzles to you. It does this by keeping a skill rating for each puzzle type, worked out on your device from how you play (for example whether you solved a puzzle, how long it took and how many hints you used). This profile never leaves your phone.
  • Coins are in-game currency earned by playing. They have no real-world value and cannot be bought or cashed out.
  • Whether you own Mind Trap Pro is remembered on your device so Pro works offline.
  • When you tap Share, the game opens your phone's share menu with a short text summary of your result. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app to share it with.
  • If Android's device backup is turned on, your phone may include Mind Trap's game data in your personal Google backup, under Google's policies. We cannot access that backup.

2. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: used by the advertising SDK to load ads and by Google Play to process and restore the Pro purchase (see below). The game itself works fully offline.
  • Advertising ID: used by the advertising SDK, as described below.
  • Google Play billing (com.android.vending.BILLING): lets the game open Google Play's own purchase screen for Mind Trap Pro.
  • Mind Trap does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or location.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

If you have not bought Mind Trap Pro, the game shows ads provided by Google AdMob: banners on some menu screens, occasional full-screen ads between levels, and optional reward videos you can choose to watch for a free hint or to skip a level. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share, under Google's own policies:

  • Device identifiers such as the advertising ID,
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • App interactions with ads, and diagnostic and performance data.

This data is used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, and is encrypted in transit. Mind Trap asks Google to show only ads suitable for general audiences (rated PG or lower).

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some US states), Mind Trap asks for your consent before any ad is requested, using Google's consent form. Where this applies, you can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings › Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

With Mind Trap Pro, no ads are shown and the game does not start the advertising SDK.

Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

4. In-app purchase (Mind Trap Pro)

Mind Trap Pro is an optional, one-time purchase. It removes ads and unlocks unlimited hints, free level skips, unlimited Brain Blitz, the Practice Lab, premium themes and the Streak Shield. It is not a subscription. The purchase is made through Google Play Billing:

  • Payment is handled entirely by Google. Your card or other payment details are entered into Google Play, never into Mind Trap, and we never receive them.
  • After a purchase, Google Play tells the game on your device which product you own (the Pro product ID and a purchase token), so the game can unlock Pro and confirm the purchase to Google. This happens on your device; the game does not send it to us.
  • To restore Pro on a new or reset phone, and to check that the purchase is still valid (for example after a refund), the game asks Google Play which purchases belong to the Google account signed in on the device.
  • As the seller, we receive the order records Google Play provides to developers (such as the order number, date, product, price, country and order status), which we use only for refunds, customer support, accounting and tax obligations.

Google processes purchase information under the Google Payments Privacy Notice and the Google Privacy Policy. Refund requests are handled through Google Play.

5. Data we collect

We, the developer of Mind Trap, do not collect, store or sell any personal data. We have no account system, analytics service or server that receives information from the game. The only information we receive is the Google Play order record described in section 4, and only if you buy Mind Trap Pro.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your game data remains on your device until you delete it. You can erase it at any time in the game under Settings › Reset all progress (this does not remove your Pro purchase). Uninstalling Mind Trap, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, also removes all of its data from your device. Your Pro purchase stays linked to your Google account and can be restored after reinstalling. Google Play order records are kept as long as required for accounting and tax purposes.

7. Children

Mind Trap is a puzzle game for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Security

Your game data is kept in the game's private storage, which other apps cannot read.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
