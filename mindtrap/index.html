Mind Trap

Mind Trap: The Impossible Puzzle

Short, surprising puzzles that fight back. Logic, physics, memory, perception and trick questions, and a game that learns which ones challenge you most.

Privacy policy Contact

How to play

  • Every level is a different kind of puzzle. A short tip and a pointer show you what to do the first time you meet each one.
  • Solve it as fast and as cleanly as you can to earn up to three stars.
  • Stuck? Use a hint, or tap ? to see how that puzzle works again.
  • In Mind Traps, nothing is what it seems: read carefully, tap anything, drag things around.

Features

  • Seven puzzle types: Ball Sort, Odd One Out, Mind Traps, Lights Out, Number Patterns, Memory Grid and Slide Shift.
  • Adaptive difficulty: the game tracks your skill in each puzzle type and tunes every level to keep you challenged.
  • Your Brain Profile shows your strongest skills and what to train next.
  • A new Daily Challenge every day, the same for every player, with streaks and a shareable result.
  • Endless journey with stars, coins, hints and achievements.
  • Colour-blind mode, sound effects and haptics.
  • Fully playable offline. Your progress is saved on your phone.
