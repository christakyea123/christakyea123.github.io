Privacy Policy

TileQuest – Tile Matching Puzzle · Last updated: 18 October 2026

This policy explains what data the TileQuest mobile game ("TileQuest", "the game", "we") handles and why. In short: your game data stays on your device. TileQuest has no user accounts and no servers of its own, and we do not receive any information about you. The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob; the ads are the only part of the game that uses the internet.

1. Your game data

  • Your progress, scores, stars, coins, boosters, unlocked themes, achievements, missions and settings are stored only on your device, inside the game's private storage.
  • The display name and avatar you choose are stored on your device only. They are shown in the game's offline practice league, whose rivals are sample players generated on your device – your name is never sent to other players or to us.
  • Coins are in-game currency earned by playing. They have no real-world value and cannot be bought or cashed out. TileQuest has no in-app purchases.
  • If Android's device backup is turned on, your phone may include TileQuest's game data in your personal Google backup, under Google's policies. We cannot access that backup.

2. Permissions

  • Internet and network state – used only by the advertising SDK to load ads (see below). The game itself works fully offline.
  • TileQuest does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or location.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

TileQuest shows ads provided by Google AdMob: optional reward videos (for example to double your coins or continue a level) and occasional full-screen ads between levels. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share, under Google's own policies:

  • Device identifiers such as the advertising ID,
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • App interactions with ads, and diagnostic and performance data.

This data is used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, and is encrypted in transit. TileQuest asks Google to show only ads suitable for general audiences (rated PG or lower).

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some US states), TileQuest asks for your consent before any ad is requested, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings › Ad privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

4. Data we collect

We, the developer of TileQuest, do not collect, store or sell any personal data. We have no account system, analytics service or server that receives information from the game.

5. Data retention and deletion

Your game data remains on your device until you delete it. Uninstalling TileQuest, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, removes all of its data from your device.

6. Children

TileQuest is a puzzle game for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Security

Your game data is kept in the game's private storage, which other apps cannot read.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
