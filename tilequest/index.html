TileQuest – Tile Matching Puzzle

Match three. Clear the board. A colourful, relaxing tile puzzle you can play anywhere – no internet needed.

Privacy policy Contact

How to play

  • Tap a tile to move it into the tray at the top.
  • Collect three of the same tile to clear them from the tray.
  • Shaded tiles are covered – clear the tiles on top first.
  • Clear the whole board before the tray fills up to win.

Features

  • Hundreds of levels across themed chapters, and you can pick any level you like.
  • Special levels: Boss boards, Golden tiles, Mystery tiles and Timed challenges.
  • A new Daily Puzzle every day, plus daily gifts, a lucky spin and daily missions.
  • Boosters: Undo, Hint, Shuffle, an extra tray slot and Sweep.
  • Combos, stars, a Star Chest, achievements and a weekly practice league.
  • Unlockable tile themes, sound effects and music.
  • Fully playable offline. Your progress is saved on your phone.
