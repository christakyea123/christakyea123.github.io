Privacy Policy

LifePocket · Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains what information LifePocket ("LifePocket", "the app", "we", "us"), developed by Christopher Akyea, handles, why, and the choices you have.

The short version
  • Your tasks, notes, journal and other entries belong to you. We use them only to run the app for you.
  • We do not sell your data, and we don't use analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • Text is sent to Google's Gemini AI only when you use an AI feature, and only the text that feature needs.
  • Cycle-tracking data is never used for ads, never sent to AI and never shared.
  • Free users see ads from Google AdMob. Premium subscribers see no ads.
  • You can export your data or delete your account at any time from inside the app.

1. Information you provide

Account information

  • Email sign-up: your email address, a password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider), and the name you choose.
  • Sign in with Google: your name, email address and Google account identifier, as provided by Google when you approve the sign-in.
  • Guest mode: an anonymous account with no email or name, so your entries can be backed up. You can upgrade it to a full account later and keep your data.

Profile photo (optional)

  • You can add a profile photo by choosing one picture with Android's photo picker or by taking one with the camera. The app only receives the single photo you pick. It never browses your gallery.
  • The photo is resized and saved on your phone. When you are signed in, it is also uploaded to our cloud storage (Supabase Storage) so it appears on your other devices. It is stored under a hard-to-guess link that only the app uses.
  • If you sign in with Google and don't add a photo, the app shows your Google profile picture.
  • You can remove your photo at any time (Profile → tap your photo → Remove photo). It is also deleted when you delete your account.

Content you create

Everything you enter to organise your life, such as tasks and subtasks, reminders, calendar events, notes, Life Inbox entries, habits, goals and milestones, routines, journal entries, mood check-ins, memories, people and check-ins, decisions, checklists, focus sessions, reviews, day plans, energy check-ins and, if you turn it on, cycle-tracking logs. Your app settings and preferences are stored too.

2. How your data is stored and synced

  • On your device first: LifePocket works offline. Your entries are saved in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • Cloud sync: when you are signed in (including guest mode), your entries are synced to our cloud database so they are backed up and available on your other devices. The database is provided by Supabase.
  • Only you can access your rows: every table is protected by row-level security tied to your account, so other users can never read your data.
  • Security: data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Your sign-in session is stored in your phone's secure storage (Android Keystore).

3. AI features (Google Gemini)

  • AI features (such as organising your Life Inbox, planning your day, breaking down tasks, goal plans, routines, checklists and summaries) run only when you start them.
  • When you do, the text needed for that request is sent from our server to the Google Gemini API to generate the response. The AI key is kept on our server and never stored in the app.
  • Your memories and journal are included only if you allow it under Settings → AI (both can be switched off). AI features can be turned off entirely.
  • Cycle-tracking data is never sent to AI.
  • We use Google's paid Gemini API service, under which Google does not use your prompts or responses to improve its products. Google processes them under the Gemini API Terms.
  • We do not store the text you send to AI or the AI's answers on our servers, except where you choose to save a result into your own entries. To enforce daily limits we keep a usage record (which feature, the model, token counts, timing and whether it succeeded) for 90 days.
  • AI suggestions can be wrong. Always review them before relying on them.

4. Cycle tracking (health information)

  • Cycle tracking is optional and off until you turn it on.
  • Period dates, flow, symptoms, mood and notes are stored on your device and synced to your account like your other entries, so they are protected the same way.
  • This data is never used for advertising, never sent to AI and never shared with anyone.
  • Reminders are discreet by default and never mention periods or fertility on your lock screen unless you change that setting.
  • Predictions are estimates and are not medical advice or a method of contraception.
  • You can erase all cycle data at any time in Cycle → Settings → Delete all cycle data.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

  • Free users see ads served by Google AdMob. Premium subscribers never see ads.
  • AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and information such as IP address and device type to show and measure ads, and personalised ads where permitted. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before ads are personalised, using Google's consent tools. You can change your choice later in the app's settings.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads on most Android phones).
  • Rewarded ads: if you choose to watch an ad for a bonus AI action, Google sends our server a confirmation that includes your account identifier, so the reward can be credited to your account.
  • Your entries (tasks, notes, journal, cycle data and so on) are never used to target ads.

6. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

  • LifePocket Premium is a subscription sold through Google Play. Google handles payment, and we never see your card details.
  • To unlock Premium, we receive the purchase token and subscription status from Google Play and verify it on our server. We store your subscription status (for example active or expired, expiry date and renewal state) with your account.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription in the Google Play Store app.

7. Permissions the app uses

  • Internet and network state (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): to sync, sign in, use AI features, load ads and process purchases.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): to show the reminders, task alerts and nudges you set up.
  • Exact alarms and restart (USE_EXACT_ALARM, SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): so reminders ring at the exact time, even after your phone restarts.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE): for alert vibration and haptic feedback, which you can turn off.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID): used by AdMob as described in section 5.
  • Photos and camera: only when you choose to set a profile photo. The app uses Android's photo picker or camera app to get the one photo you select, and does not request access to your photo library or files.
  • The app does not access your microphone, contacts, location or call logs.

8. Who we share data with

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with service providers that run the app for us, under their own privacy and security commitments:

  • Supabase: authentication, database and server functions (hosting your synced data).
  • Google: Sign in with Google, the Gemini API (AI features), AdMob (ads for free users) and Google Play Billing (subscriptions).

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users or the app.

9. How long we keep data

  • Your entries are kept while your account exists, until you delete them.
  • Deleted entries are removed from the cloud permanently 60 days after you delete them (this lets deletions sync to your other devices).
  • AI usage records are deleted after 90 days.
  • When you delete your account, your account, all synced data and your profile photo are deleted from our servers. Signing out also erases the data stored on that phone.

10. Your choices and rights

  • Export your data: Profile → Settings → Export my data.
  • Delete your account: Profile → Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and all synced data.
  • Can't access the app? Email christakyea123@gmail.com from the address on your account with the subject "LifePocket account deletion", and we will delete your account and data within 30 days.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, or to object to or restrict certain processing. Contact us to exercise these rights.

11. Children

LifePocket is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. International transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than yours. Where required, they use appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We will update the date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.

14. Contact
