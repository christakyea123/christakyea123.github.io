Cyber Rush: Future Collapse

Next-Generation 3D Futuristic Endless Runner

Sprint through a shattered cyberpunk metropolis, evade high-voltage laser barricades, outmaneuver the colossal Harbinger Mech, and upgrade tactical power suits in a world where speed is survival.

Core Gameplay & Controls

  • Swipe Left / Right: Seamless 3-lane lateral evasion with dynamic camera tracking.
  • Swipe Up: Fluid parabolic jump over ground hazards, barricades, and low laser tripwires.
  • Swipe Down: Fast-dive ground slide with compressed hitboxes to glide under high laser gates.
  • Double Tap: Trigger suit-specific tactical overdrive abilities.

Key Features

  • Giant Robot Pursuit: Face the terrifying *Harbinger Mech* as it pursues you across neon highways, crushing obstacles in its wake and launching devastating ground slams and laser sweeps.
  • Procedural Endless City: Race through dynamically generated $40\text{m}$ city segments featuring neon billboards, towering cyber skyscrapers, and abandoned futuristic vehicles.
  • Guaranteed Fairness: Smart procedural generation ensures every hazard combination has at least one fair and achievable path.
  • Tactical Exosuit Armory:
    • Cyber Operative: High-agility standard chassis with built-in Energy Shield.
    • Titan Enforcer: Reinforced heavy plating for maximum kinetic impact absorption.
    • Neon Phantom: Lightweight speed frame with extended Quantum Dash burst.
    • Chrono Vanguard: Equipped with temporal warp dampeners that slow hazardous environments.
  • Daily Missions & Achievements: Complete daily tactical objectives to earn Energy Coins and unlock custom suit upgrades.
  • Performance Engine: Optimized for 60 FPS mobile performance with customizable Low, Medium, High, and Ultra graphics presets.
  • 100% Playable Offline: All core gameplay, progression, and local records are preserved without requiring an active internet connection.
