Cyber Rush: Future Collapse

Outrun the collapse. Topple the giants.

Sprint down an endless neon city that is glitching out of existence while a towering Colossus stomps ahead of you, smashing lanes, raining missiles and sweeping its eye-laser across the street. Charge your Overdrive, break its power cores and bring it down.

How to Play

  • Swipe left / right: switch between three lanes.
  • Swipe up: jump over barriers and low laser beams.
  • Swipe down: slide under gates and high lasers.
  • Overdrive: collect orange energy cells, then tap Overdrive to blast the Colossus and smash through obstacles.

Features

  • Endless Run: an infinite avenue with six city sectors that change as you go, plus records to beat.
  • Six Colossal Bosses: Harbinger, Warden, Tempest, Hydra, Juggernaut and Omega, each with lane slams, missile storms and laser sweeps.
  • Boss Raids: a bonus mode where you face each Colossus head-on and shoot out its weak-point cores.
  • Power-ups: shield, credit magnet, score x2 and energy cells.
  • Upgrades and Exosuits: upgrade your gear and unlock Vanguard, Crimson Ronin, Spectre and Aurum Prime.
  • Daily Ops, Missions and Achievements: new goals and rewards every day, plus a 7-day login bonus.
  • Plays Offline: all gameplay and progress work without an internet connection.
