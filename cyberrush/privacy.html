Privacy Policy

Cyber Rush: Future Collapse · Last updated: 22 November 2026

This privacy policy explains what information is handled when you download and play Cyber Rush: Future Collapse ("Cyber Rush", "the game", "we", "us"), developed by Christopher Akyea.

In short: your game data stays on your device. Cyber Rush has no user accounts, no registration and no game servers of its own. We do not collect, view or sell your personal information. The game uses two Google services: Google AdMob for advertising and Google Play Billing for optional in-app purchases.

1. Game Data Stored on Your Device

  • Progress and statistics: best run distance and score, credits and data cores, unlocked weapons (Pulse Rifle, Scatter Cannon, Rail Lance), exosuits (Vanguard, Crimson Ronin, Spectre, Aurum Prime), upgrades, Boss Raid stars, daily missions, achievements, login streak and settings (audio, vibration, graphics, aim assist) are saved only on your phone in the game's private app storage.
  • No cloud sync: the game works fully offline and your progress is never uploaded to the developer.
  • Virtual currency: credits and data cores are virtual game items with no real-world monetary value and cannot be exchanged for money.

2. Permissions

  • Internet and network state (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to load ads and by Google Play Billing to process purchases.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): used by Google AdMob as described in Section 3.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE): short haptic feedback when you crash or land big hits. You can turn it off in the game's Settings.
  • The game does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files, precise location or phone calls.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Cyber Rush is free to play and shows ads provided by Google AdMob:

  • Banner ads on menu screens only, never during gameplay.
  • Interstitial ads between runs, limited to at most one every three runs and never more than once every few minutes.
  • Rewarded video ads that you choose to watch for a bonus, for example to revive after a crash, double the credits from a run, double a daily login reward or claim a free supply drop.

To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, and diagnostic and ad-interaction data, in line with Google's policies:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

4. Your Privacy Choices and Consent

  • Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and some US states), the game shows Google's consent form before personalized ads are requested.
  • You can change your choice at any time from Settings › Ad Privacy Choices inside the game.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings (Settings › Google › Ads or Settings › Privacy › Ads).
  • Buying Remove Ads or the Starter Pack turns off banner and interstitial ads permanently.

5. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional purchases (Remove Ads, Starter Pack, data cores and credits) are processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive or store your payment card details, name or billing address. Google Play may share a purchase confirmation with the game so that the item can be delivered. Purchases are subject to the Google Play Terms of Service.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your progress is stored only on your device, uninstalling Cyber Rush or clearing the app's storage in Android settings permanently deletes it. You can also erase it in the game from Settings › Reset Progress. Data collected by Google for advertising is governed by Google's retention policies.

7. Children's Privacy

Cyber Rush: Future Collapse is designed for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Security

Save data is kept in Android's private, sandboxed app storage, which other apps cannot read or modify.

9. Changes to This Policy

We will post any changes on this page and update the date at the top.
