Privacy Policy

Cyber Rush: Future Collapse · Last updated: 18 November 2026

This privacy policy explains what information is handled when you download and play Cyber Rush: Future Collapse ("Cyber Rush", "the game", "we", "us"), developed by Christopher Akyea.

In short: Your game data stays 100% on your device. Cyber Rush has no user registration, no mandatory online accounts, and no backend servers of its own. We do not collect, view, or sell any personal information. Advertising in the game is served by Google AdMob, which is the only component of the application requiring an internet connection.

1. Local Game Data & Storage

  • Game Progress & Stats: Your high scores, distance traveled, energy coins collected, unlocked power suits (Cyber Operative, Titan Enforcer, Neon Phantom, Chrono Vanguard), suit upgrades, daily challenge records, and custom settings (graphics quality, frame rates, audio levels) are stored strictly locally on your phone in the game's secure sandboxed storage.
  • No Cloud Accounts Required: The game is fully functional offline. Your progress does not synchronize to any private developer servers.
  • Energy Coins: All currency and upgrades in Cyber Rush are virtual game items earned purely by playing or watching optional rewarded ads. They hold no real-world monetary value and cannot be redeemed for real money.

2. Permissions Requested

  • Internet and Network State (android.permission.INTERNET, android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used exclusively by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to request and display advertisements, check network availability, and verify rewarded video ad completions.
  • Advertising ID: Used by Google AdMob as described in Section 3 below.
  • Sensory & Device Privacy: Cyber Rush does not request or require access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, storage files, location GPS, or phone state.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Cyber Rush displays ads provided by Google AdMob:

  • Banner Ads: Shown on selected non-gameplay screens, such as the bottom of the Main Menu or Suit Armory. Banners are never displayed during active running gameplay to ensure controls remain unobstructed.
  • Interstitial Ads: Full-screen transition advertisements shown between game sessions. These are frequency-capped to appear at most once every three completed runs.
  • Rewarded Video Ads: Optional video advertisements you can choose to watch to gain an in-game reward (for example, reviving your character once after a crash with a shield, or doubling coins collected during a run).

To serve, measure, and protect against fraudulent ad traffic, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy, including:

  • Device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID),
  • Approximate geographical location derived from IP address,
  • Diagnostic, performance, and ad interaction logs.

For more details on how Google manages advertising information, please review: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. User Privacy Choices & Consent

Where required by applicable data protection laws (such as in the European Economic Area under GDPR, the UK, or relevant US state privacy regulations):

  • Consent is requested using Google's certified consent management platform before personalized ads are served.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time through your Android device settings under Settings › Google › Ads.
  • You can play Cyber Rush completely offline with all gameplay mechanics, suit switching, missions, and progression fully functional.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all gameplay progress is kept locally on your hardware, uninstalling Cyber Rush: Future Collapse or clearing the application's storage in your Android device settings permanently deletes all save files, scores, and unlocked suits from your device.

6. Children's Privacy

Cyber Rush: Future Collapse is designed for a general audience and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children.

7. Security

Local save data is protected by the Android sandbox operating environment, which prevents other applications from accessing or modifying your game files.

8. Policy Updates

Any revisions to this privacy policy will be posted on this page with an updated modification date.
