What Scannax does

  • Scan with your camera, with automatic page edges and crop.
  • Enhance makes the paper white and the text sharp.
  • Recognise text (OCR) on your phone, copy it or export to Word.
  • Sign, merge, split, compress and password-protect PDFs.
  • Share small PDFs, or save pages to your photo gallery.
  • No account and no document cloud: your files never leave your device unless you share them.
